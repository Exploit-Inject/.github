### ◈ About ExploitInject

Headquartered in **Bangladesh** and operating globally, **ExploitInject** is a premier technology startup and engineering collective. We fuse the offensive precision of **Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing** with the architectural craftsmanship of **Modern Software Engineering** to deliver robust, high-performance, and battle-hardened digital solutions.

> #### ⚡ *"Breaking Barriers, Securing Systems"*

### 🔒 Security & Responsible Disclosure

At ExploitInject, digital security is at the core of everything we build. If you discover a security vulnerability within any of our repositories, platforms, or services:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Send an encrypted / confidential report directly to **[`admin@exploitinject.dev`](mailto:admin@exploitinject.dev)** or **[`support@exploitinject.dev`](mailto:support@exploitinject.dev)**.
3. Include reproducible proof-of-concept steps, affected endpoints, and potential impact.
4. We acknowledge all legitimate disclosures within 48 hours and work expeditiously on verified mitigations.
